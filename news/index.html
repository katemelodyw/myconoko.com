Mushroom Cultivation Technology

Myconoko developed the Mobile Mushroom Farm that can be set up anywhere - in urban centres, office buildings and even homes. The system includes a moisture retaining chamber, LED lights, waterproof circulating fan, exhaust and spore filter, webcam and shelfing - all controlled by an IoT system that you can manage on your mobile device. The basic 1m x 1m system can grow up to 8kg of mushrooms per month, using Myconoko's own high-yielding substrates made from local recycled wood and food waste, right here at Myconoko's Mushroom Lab in Hong Kong.

Mushroom Grow Bloc: Modular Desktop Mushroom Farm

To debut later this year is the desktop version of the Mobile Mushroom Farm. This is a stackable system where each module is measured 40cm x 30cm x 30cm, and is perfect for your kitchen counter, school science room, or restaurant or office shelf. It is equipped with an automated humidifier, and optional LED. When you're ready to expand you can simply add more Mushroom Grow Blocs and stack them however you like. Designed to house Myconoko's Mushroom Home Kits for those who want a hassle-free and success-guaranteed way to grow your own mushrooms.

Bespoke Mushroom Environments

From large mushroom farms to artistic mushroom environments, so long as you have a vision, we can do it. Pictured above is a bespoke mushroom terrarium designed and installed in collaboration with artist Zheng Mahler at the PHD Gallery in Wan Chai, Hong Kong, between March and July 2026. Envisioned as a microcosm of Lantau's ecology, Myconoko designed the environmental conditions from humidity and airflow to shade and positioning ideal for the symbiosis between plants and fungi. Mushrooms that are seen throughout the exhibition were also cultivated by Myconoko, and were later seen to thrive and fruit on their own in their new environment.
