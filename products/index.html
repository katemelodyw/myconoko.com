Mushroom Home Kit

Grow your own mushrooms in 3 weeks!

A simple, beginner-friendly home growing kit for fresh Oyster and Lion's Mane mushrooms, prized for their umami-rich flavour and medicinal value. Designed for ease, convenience, and enjoyment, our Home Kit offers a fun way to grow, harvest, and savour gourmet mushrooms right from home.

Each Home Kit is approximately 500g or 1000g, available in a 1L tub or 2L tub, and contains sterilised substrate made from recycled wood chips from Hong Kong, organic-certified food waste compost from Hong Kong, gypsum, water, Oyster mushroom spawn made in Hong Kong, cloth, and a mushroom growing and cooking guide.

Detailed growing instruction booklet here.

  • [HKSO1/HKSO2] Shimeji Oyster Mushroom Home Kit (1L/2L): HKD150 / HKD250
  • [HKGO1/HKGO2] Golden Oyster Mushroom Home Kit (1L/2L): HKD150 / HKD250
  • [HKPO1/HKPO2] Pink Oyster Mushroom Home Kit (1L/2L): HKD150 / HKD250
  • [HKLM2] Lion's Mane Mushroom Home Kit (2L): HKD250

*Prices exclude delivery.

Our Home Kit is suitable for beginners and can be enjoyed as a hands-on introduction to mushroom cultivation at home.

Place the kit in a suitable indoor growing area and follow the included instructions for watering, harvesting, and cooking.
