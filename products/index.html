Potent Fungi Mushroom Tinctures & Concentrates

Myconoko is the sole Hong Kong distributor of Potent Fungi, a premium range of potent, sugar-free functional mushroom extracts made in Chiang Mai, Thailand. Produced in an FDA-registered laboratory, each formula is dual-extracted using organic fruiting bodies only and is available in Lion’s Mane, Cordyceps, and Reishi (Lingzhi) varieties.

Lion's Mane for FOCUS and mental clarity - Lion's Mane makes a family of compounds called hericenones, found in the fruiting body and almost nowhere else in nature. In laboratory research hericenones prompt nerve growth factor, the protein your brain uses to build and maintain the connections that hold attention and memory.

Cordyceps for sustained ENERGY that doesn't crash - Cordyceps militaris is the cordycepin-rich cousin of the wild Himalayan fungus. Cordycepin is a near-twin of adenosine, the molecule your cells use to carry energy, which is the leading idea for how Cordyceps supports stamina.

Reishi (Lingzhi) for CALM and better sleep - Reishi carries two families of compounds. The triterpenes, the bitter ganoderic acids, dissolve in alcohol. The beta-glucans dissolve in water. A single extraction leaves half of Reishi behind and that is why they are dual-extracted. The triterpenes are the ones studied for calm and stress adaptation. The beta glucans are what your immune cells read. Together they are the reason Reishi has been valued for rest and resilience for two thousand years.
