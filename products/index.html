Mushroom Home Kit - Grow your own mushrooms in 3 weeks! A simple, beginner-friendly home growing kit for fresh Oyster and Lion's Mane mushrooms, prized for their umami-rich flavour and medicinal value. Designed for ease, convenience, and enjoyment, our Home Kit offers a fun way to grow, harvest, and savour gourmet mushrooms right from home. Each Home Kit is approx. 500g/1000g (1L tub/2L tub) and contains: Sterilised substrate (recycled wood chips from Hong Kong, organic certified food waste compost from Hong Kong, gypsum, water), Oyster Mushroom spawn made in Hong Kong, cloth, mushroom growing an cooking guide.
